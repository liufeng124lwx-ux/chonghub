# Refero research synthesis

Reviewed style references: Shop.app, RevenueCat, Fiverr. Reviewed concrete screens: FlowMapp pricing, AVNIER product details, Fourthwall landing.

- Shop.app: compact navigation, white canvas, product-first cards, contained imagery, one accent for interaction.
- RevenueCat: single accent role, spacious hierarchy, sharp informational surfaces, CTA reserved for actions.
- Fiverr: service marketplace grouping, clear category/fit signals, task-oriented hero and cards.
- FlowMapp: pricing sequence of headline, billing/selection controls, plan cards, comparison table, FAQ, footer. Adapted without annual/monthly toggle or free-trial claims.
- AVNIER: slim utility header, two-column product detail, selectors and price before full-width CTA, service reassurance strip. Adapted without cart or online payment.
- Fourthwall: strong marketing navigation, single primary CTA, product proof and FAQ/testimonial modules. Adapted without invented social proof.

Recommendation: keep ChongHub's current light/teal system, reduce navigation, make `/products` the catalog plus pricing surface, and put the existing manual purchase flow inside a clearer two-column product detail hierarchy.
