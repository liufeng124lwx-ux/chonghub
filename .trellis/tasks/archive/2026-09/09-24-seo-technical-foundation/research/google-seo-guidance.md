# Google SEO guidance used for this task

- Sitemap helps discovery but does not guarantee indexing or ranking: https://developers.google.com/search/help/crawling-index-faq
- `Sitemap` in robots.txt should be a fully qualified URL: https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/robots-txt/robots-txt-spec
- `noindex` must be crawlable; robots.txt disallow prevents Google from seeing the directive: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/block-indexing
- Product structured data should focus on single product pages and use real offer data: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product
- FAQ rich results are restricted for most non-government/non-health sites: https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/08/howto-faq-changes
