# Content guidance used for this task

- Google recommends helpful, reliable, people-first content rather than search-engine-first pages: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- Keyword stuffing is a spam practice; the implementation must not enforce a fixed density: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
- Next.js supports local MDX and dynamic routes; frontmatter requires an explicit parser/plugin or exported metadata: https://nextjs.org/docs/app/guides/mdx
